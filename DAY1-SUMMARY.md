## What each section covers

### 🧮 Math — Kimchi Kitchen Math Lab
Fractions, ratios, percentages, unit rates, area/volume, mean/median/range, BEDMAS, elapsed time — themed around kimchi recipes, bibimbap orders, bulgogi, and restaurant prep. 5 worked examples + **16 interactive problems** with hints and solutions.

### 📖 Reading — A Table Full of Stories
~520-word passage on Korean food culture (banchan, kimchi/fermentation, bibimbap, bulgogi, etiquette, geography). Comprehension quiz, open response with model answer, vocabulary match + fill-in.

### ✍️ Writing — Descriptive Writing (A Korean Meal)
Sensory descriptive paragraph (restaurant / first bite / shared table). Spot strong vs plain description, Grammar Clinic (Canadian spelling), then write 150–230 words with checklist, download/print.

### 🇰🇷 Korean — Food & greetings (10 words, ALL with audio)
Hangul + Revised Romanization + English + example sentence. For **every** word: Hear model (ko-KR-SunHiNeural MP3), Record, Play my try, Play model. Also: matching, situation MC, type romanization, click-to-reveal.
