🧮 Math Day 2: Chuseok Family Math

This week, Jacqueline, the whole family is heading home for Chuseok! You'll use unit rates, fractions, percentages (including percent decrease), three-part ratios, circle area, map scales, mean/median/range, equations, BEDMAS, money, and elapsed time to plan travel, bake songpyeon, and figure out family ages.

Part 1: Learn the Moves

The 4-Step Problem Solver:
  1. Read: What is the question actually asking? Underline numbers and units.
  2. Plan: Which operations, and in what order?
  3. Solve: One step at a time; write each step down.
  4. Check: Is the answer reasonable? Did you answer the question asked?

Worked Example 1: Songpyeon unit rate

2 cups of rice flour make 30 songpyeon. How many cups for 75 songpyeon?

Step 1: Unit rate = 30 ÷ 2 = 15 songpyeon per cup.

Step 2: 75 ÷ 15 = 5 cups.

Worked Example 2: A three-part ratio

Fillings of sesame, chestnut, and red bean are in a 4 : 3 : 1 ratio. The family makes 48 songpyeon. How many are red bean?

Total parts = 4 + 3 + 1 = 8. One part = 48 ÷ 8 = 6.

Red bean = 1 part = 6 songpyeon (sesame 24, chestnut 18).

Worked Example 3: Percent decrease

A gift box of Korean pears drops from $50 to $40 after the holiday. What is the percent decrease?

Decrease = 50 − 40 = 10. Percent decrease = 10 ÷ original 50 = 0.20 = 20%.

Key idea: always divide by the starting amount.

Worked Example 4: Area of a circle (the full moon!)

A round serving tray has a radius of 10 cm. Use π ≈ 3.14.

Area = π × r² = 3.14 × 10 × 10 = 314 cm².

Careful: if you're given the diameter, halve it first to get the radius.

Worked Example 5: Ages as an equation

Halmeoni is 84. Her age is 4 more than twice Eomma's age. Write it as an equation with Eomma's age m: 2m + 4 = 84.

Subtract 4: 2m = 80. Divide by 2: m = 40. Check: 2 × 40 + 4 = 84 ✔
