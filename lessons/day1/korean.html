🇰🇷 Korean Day 1: Food & Greetings

Jacqueline, welcome to beginner Korean! Today — while your English lessons explore Korean food and culture — you'll learn 10 essential words with Hangul, Revised Romanization, English meaning, and listen-and-say practice for every word.

How to read Revised Romanization:
  • Letters approximate the sound in English. Korean does not use tone numbers like Cantonese.
  • Example: annyeonghaseyo — roughly ahn-nyoung-ha-seh-yo.
  • Listening tip: Tap Hear model, say the word out loud three times, then Record and compare.
