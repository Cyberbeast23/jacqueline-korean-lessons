🇰🇷 Korean Day 3: Hangeul Day & School

Welcome back, Jacqueline! October 9 was 한글날 (Hangeul Day), when Korea celebrates its own alphabet. This week you'll learn 10 new school words with Hangul, Revised Romanization, English meaning, and listen-and-say practice for every word. Example sentences reuse earlier words (안녕하세요, 주세요, 맛있어요) so you keep them fresh.

How to read Revised Romanization:
  • Letters approximate the sound in English. Korean does not use tone numbers like Cantonese.
  • Example: seonsaengnim — roughly sun-saeng-nim. The letters eo sound like the "u" in up, and ae sounds like the "e" in bed.
  • Sound change: 한글날 is written han-geul-nal but said hangeullal, because ㄹ + ㄴ turns into an "ll" sound.
  • Listening tip: Tap Hear model, say the word out loud three times, then Record and compare.

Culture tip: Korean students call a teacher 선생님 (seonsaengnim), never by first name. The ending -님 (-nim) adds respect. Many Korean students also bow slightly when greeting a teacher, and classmates often take turns cleaning their own classroom at the end of the day.
