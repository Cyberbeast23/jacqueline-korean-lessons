🧮 Math Day 4: Seoul Travel Math

안녕하세요, Jacqueline! This week you're a traveller in Seoul. You'll use multiplication with large numbers, currency conversion (won ↔ Canadian dollars), percent fees and discounts, average speed, timetables, elapsed time, unit rates, map scale, equations, and the mean to plan subway rides, train trips, and a day budget.

Part 1: Learn the Moves

The 4-Step Problem Solver:
  1. Read: What is the question actually asking? Underline numbers and units.
  2. Plan: Which operations, and in what order?
  3. Solve: One step at a time; write each step down.
  4. Check: Is the answer reasonable? Did you answer the question asked?

Worked Example 1: Converting currency

Korea uses the won (₩, KRW). In this lesson we use an example rate of 1 CAD = 1,000 KRW (real rates change every day). A bowl of bibimbap costs ₩11,500. How much is that in Canadian dollars?

Won → dollars: divide by 1,000. 11,500 ÷ 1,000 = $11.50 CAD.

Dollars → won: multiply by 1,000. So $8 CAD = ₩8,000.

Worked Example 2: Average speed

An airport bus travels 60 km in 1 hour 30 minutes. What is its average speed?

Change the time to hours: 1 h 30 min = 1.5 h.

Speed = distance ÷ time = 60 ÷ 1.5 = 40 km/h.

Worked Example 3: Reading a repeating timetable

A bus leaves the station at 7:10 a.m. and then every 25 minutes. You reach the stop at 8:10 a.m. When is the next bus, and how long do you wait?

7:10 → 7:35 → 8:00 → 8:25. The 8:00 bus has already gone, so the next one is 8:25: a 15-minute wait.

Tip: keep adding the gap and write each time down so you don't skip one.

Worked Example 4: Percent fee

An exchange booth gives you ₩100,000 but keeps a 3% fee. How many won do you actually receive?

Fee = 3% × 100,000 = 0.03 × 100,000 = ₩3,000.

You receive 100,000 − 3,000 = ₩97,000. (Or: 97% × 100,000.)

Worked Example 5: Elapsed time across noon

A train leaves at 10:50 a.m. and arrives at 1:35 p.m. How long is the trip?

10:50 → 12:50 is 2 hours. 12:50 → 1:35 is 45 minutes.

Total = 2 h 45 min = 2 × 60 + 45 = 165 minutes.
